## Overview

Hack The Box’s Dante Pro Lab was an intensive experience that allowed me to test my penetration testing skills. Consisting of a simulated corporate network with 14 machines and 27 flags, the lab covers several areas of attack and defense, including:

- Information enumeration and gathering
- Vulnerability exploitation
- Privilege escalation
- Lateral movement
- Web application attacks

In this lab, I faced several practical challenges, including using advanced pivoting techniques to bypass defenses. Although the Dante Pro Lab is designed to be a challenge for beginners, it requires a solid foundation of practical knowledge.

---

## Personal Experience

I completed the **Dante Pro Lab** in 3 super intense days, an experience that pushed me to use different attack techniques and tools. One of the main challenges was using **pivoting** to access internal machines, which required me to be very careful and use advanced tools such as **Ligolo-ng** to simplify the process of creating tunnels between compromised machines.

The intensity of the experience was enhanced by the need to exploit several vulnerabilities, which allowed me to sharpen my skills in privilege escalation and web application compromise.

---

## Recommended Tools

For this lab, I used some advanced tools to facilitate access to internal machines:

- **Ligolo-ng**: A useful tool for creating tunnels between compromised networks. Recommended, but it is also good to become familiar with **manual pivoting** using SSH, **port forwarding** and **chisel**.
- **Metasploit** and **LinEnum** were instrumental in gathering information and exploiting vulnerabilities on the target systems.

## Negative aspects

The lab, while very useful, has some limitations in terms of realism. Many of the machines are running outdated operating systems, which made it possible to exploit known vulnerabilities without the need for extensive research. Additionally, the network does not fully simulate a corporate environment, as the machines are not connected in a way that reflects a complex network typical of a real infrastructure.


---

## Conclusions

Despite some criticism, **Dante Pro Lab** is an experience that I highly recommend to anyone who wants to test their penetration testing skills. During my journey, I acquired practical skills that will help me deal with more complex scenarios. It is an excellent base for those who want to perfect their lateral movement, privilege escalation and vulnerability exploitation techniques.
