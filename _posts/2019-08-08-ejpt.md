## Introduction

The eJPT ( Junior Penetration Tester) is an entry-level certification designed for those with a basic understanding of networks and systems. It simulates real-world penetration testing scenarios, covering topics like Assessment Methodologies, Host and Network Auditing, Penetration Testing, and Web Application Testing. This hands-on exam is perfect for newcomers to cybersecurity, proving that the candidate has the foundational skills to contribute to a penetration testing team.

---

## Why did I choose eJPT?


When I decided to seriously approach the world of penetration testing, I looked for a certification that was designed for beginners, but with a certain desire to get their hands dirty. The eJPT (eLearnSecurity Junior Penetration Tester) seemed like the most sensible choice: a real practical introduction to pentesting, without too much theory for its own sake and, above all, with an exam based on a real environment to attack.

My background, at the time, was not academic: I had no experience in the sector yet, but I had started to train myself independently. I started with the Hack The Box training modules, and then moved on to building increasingly complex machines on HTB, TryHackMe, and VulnLab. I built a practical base on my own, learning the basics of Penetration Testing.

The eJPT, in this context, was my first real “checkpoint”: a way to validate what I was learning with a structured and recognized exam.

## Certification topics



**Host and Networking Auditing (25%)**

- Compile information from files on target
- Enumerate network information from files on target
- Enumerate system information on target
- Gather user account information on target
- Transfer files to and from target
- Gather hash/password information from target


**Host and Network Penetration Testing (35%)**
- Identify and modify exploits
- Conduct exploitation with metasploit
- Demonstrate pivoting by adding a route and by port forwarding
- Conduct brute-force password attacks and hash cracking




**Web Application Penetration Testing (15%)**

- Identify vulnerabilities in web applications
- Locate hidden file and directories
- Conduct brute-force login attack
- Conduct web application reconnaissance

## Pre-requisites for the Exam

1. Basic knowledge of Linux and Windows operating systems
2. Familiarity with Kali Linux and its tools
3. Strong understanding of TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP, and other networking protocols
4. Familiarity with IP addressing, subnetting, and routing concepts


## Preparation for the exam


When I got to the eJPT preparation, I already had a good practical base. I had spent a lot of time on the Hack The Box and TryHackMe modules. This helped me a lot because I had already put many of the concepts in the eJPT into practice in real environments.

I didn't follow the entire official eLearnSecurity course systematically: I used the modules mainly to review the methodologies, clarify the theoretical points and fill some gaps.

---

## Practical advice for those who want to tackle eJPT


**Practice on real platforms**

- If you already have experience with platforms like Hack The Box or TryHackMe, make the most of it. Practice on real environments is essential to understand attack and defense techniques.

**Familiarize yourself with the main tools**

- Make sure you are familiar with tools like Nmap, Metasploit, and Burp Suite. Mastering these tools will be very useful during the exam.

**Study attack methodologies**

- Understanding the phases of penetration testing (reconnaissance, scanning, exploitation, post-exploitation) will help you better structure your approach during the exam.

**Manage your time well during the exam**

- The exam offers enough time, but it is important to plan your activities well to avoid wasting time on secondary problems.

**Stay calm and focused**

- During the exam, you may encounter obstacles or unexpected situations. Keep calm, analyze the problem carefully and look for alternative solutions.

---

## What did eJPT really give me?

Even though I already had a good practical basis, eJPT helped me consolidate the methodologies and better structure my approach to penetration testing. It gave me a clearer view of the entire process, from information gathering to exploitation, and gave me more confidence in my professional path.
